Browser dispatch research

Root & Intent Runner

Try Android storage entry points, run the preserved intent corpus as a resumable JavaScript queue, and keep a local evidence log. The page attempts dispatch only; Android and the browser still enforce exported/BROWSABLE rules, permissions, and user confirmation.

No upload · local log
Root is not web-readable. A normal browser cannot enumerate /. These buttons ask Android for a handler or picker; they do not bypass the browser sandbox.
Automatic is best-effort. Chrome may require a fresh gesture for every external launch. The runner records blocked/no-signal attempts instead of calling them successes.
Return signals are ambiguous. Hidden/visible or blur/focus means an app or chooser may have opened. It does not prove an action completed.

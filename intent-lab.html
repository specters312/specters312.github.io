Android Intent Lab

Test a corpus of Android intent actions one at a time from Chrome, record browser fallback and page lifecycle signals, confirm outcomes manually, and export the resulting log.

Local logs only
One tap per case. Chrome blocks external-app launches from timers or scripts without a user gesture.
BROWSABLE only. A browser can invoke only matching activities that declare android.intent.category.BROWSABLE.
Evidence, not certainty. Fallback is explicit; visibility and focus signals only suggest an activity or chooser opened.

Need automatic enumeration?

The native companion automatically resolves the complete corpus, inventories its host package and WebView provider, enumerates installed packages, and includes an offline read-only filesystem explorer showing exactly what the app UID can access. It includes 475 action/URI cases generated from 229 BROWSABLE filters across 419 unique APKs preserved from the two prior firmware sets.

How detection works

Every generated URI includes Chrome's encoded S.browser_fallback_url. If Chrome returns through that URL, the record becomes fallback/no launch. If the page becomes hidden and later visible, it becomes returned after activity or chooser. Neither signal is treated as proof that a Settings operation succeeded.
